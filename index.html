WASTE GUIDE

Know Your Waste ♻️

Learn how to identify different types of waste and choose the right disposal method.

🍎

Organic / Wet Waste

Food scraps, fruits, vegetables and other biodegradable waste.

Examples: Food waste, fruit peels, vegetables, garden waste.

Tip: Keep wet waste separate for composting or appropriate organic processing.
♻️

Recyclable / Dry Waste

Materials such as paper, plastic, glass and metal that may be recyclable.

Examples: Paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, cans and glass containers.

Tip: Keep recyclable materials reasonably clean and separated from wet waste.
📱

E-Waste

Discarded electronic and electrical devices that need separate handling.

Examples: Phones, laptops, keyboards, monitors and other electronic devices.

Tip: Keep electronic items separate from ordinary household waste.
⚠️

Special Care Waste

Items that require additional care during collection and disposal.

Examples: Batteries and other items requiring special disposal guidance.

Tip: Keep these items separate and follow local authorized disposal guidance.
